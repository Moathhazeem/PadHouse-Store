PadHouse Store is a Palestinian e-commerce business focused on iPad accessories. Its app should be mobile-only, Arabic-first, and fully RTL. The official visual identity uses turquoise `#2EC4B6` with dark navy and light surfaces.

**Why:** These are product requirements from the user's brief and should guide future PadHouse work.

**How to apply:** Keep customer, AI/support, and admin experiences within the same visual identity. Treat compatibility, inventory, orders, policies, payments, AI, and automation as verified only when current store data or connected services support them; otherwise label them as demo or unavailable.
