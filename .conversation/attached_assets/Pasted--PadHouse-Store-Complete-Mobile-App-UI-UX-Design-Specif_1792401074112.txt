# PadHouse Store — Complete Mobile App UI/UX Design Specification

## 1. Project Overview

Design a complete, high-fidelity, mobile-only application for **PadHouse Store (باد هاوس ستور)**, a Palestinian e-commerce business specializing in iPad accessories.

The goal is to create a complete mobile shopping experience combined with AI-powered shopping assistance, customer support, marketing management, and workflow automation.

This is a complete application redesign from scratch. Do not simply create a home screen or a collection of disconnected mockups.

The final design must include:

1. Customer shopping application.
2. Product discovery, search, categories, and product details.
3. Cart and complete checkout journey.
4. Customer accounts and order tracking.
5. AI Accessory Finder.
6. AI Smart Chat.
7. Product Comparison.
8. AI Customer Support.
9. Role-restricted administrator experience.
10. Product, order, and customer management.
11. Campaign Management and Marketing Analytics.
12. AI Content Studio.
13. Automation Center.
14. Marketing Intelligence and AI recommendations.

All modules must belong to one coherent mobile application and use one consistent PadHouse design system.

### Platform Requirements

- Mobile application only.
- Arabic-first interface.
- Full RTL layout.
- Support both guest browsing and registered customers.
- Complete shopping and checkout user journeys.
- Administrator functionality accessible from within the same app, protected by role-based permissions.
- AI functionality integrated into the shopping and management experiences.
- Design all required screens, states, and navigation flows.

Do not generate desktop websites, desktop dashboards, or tablet-first layouts.

### 2.1 Official Brand Colors

Use this color palette throughout every screen and component.

**Primary brand color — Teal / Turquoise**

- Primary turquoise: `#2EC4B6`
- Use for primary actions, selected navigation elements, interactive highlights, active states, progress indicators, and selected controls.
- Use darker or lighter variations only where necessary for contrast and interaction states.
- Do not replace the turquoise identity with purple, blue, or a generic AI-themed palette.

**Dark neutral colors**

- Primary dark: `#0F172A`
- Secondary dark: `#1E293B`
- Use for headings, primary text, important icons, labels, and strong visual elements.

**Background colors**

- Main background: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary background: `#FAFAFA`
- Keep surfaces clean and light so product photography remains the visual focus.
- Use subtle neutral borders and restrained shadows.

**Semantic colors**

- Success and confirmation: `#10B981`
- Errors and urgent alerts: `#EF4444`
- Warnings and promotional highlights: `#F59E0B`

Use semantic colors consistently. Do not use error or warning colors decoratively.

Ensure adequate contrast between text and background colors. Use dark text on turquoise buttons when necessary for readability.

### 2.2 Typography

Use one consistent Arabic font family throughout the application.

Preferred options:

1. Cairo.
2. IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
3. Tajawal.

Prefer Cairo or IBM Plex Sans Arabic when available and appropriate.

Typography hierarchy:

- Main headings: Bold or ExtraBold.
- Section headings: Bold.
- Product names: Medium or Bold.
- Body text: Regular.
- Supporting text: Regular with readable contrast.
- Button labels: Medium or Bold.
- Captions and metadata: readable, never excessively small.

Define a reusable type scale for headings, product titles, descriptions, prices, labels, and captions.

Use correct Arabic shaping, spacing, line height, and text wrapping. Avoid awkward translations and mixed-direction layout errors.

### 2.3 Logo and Brand Assets

The PadHouse logo uses the official PadHouse wordmark with a vertical "Pad" and "House" arrangement, with turquoise or white treatment in the official circular turquoise icon where applicable.

- Use the official logo asset whenever available.
- Preserve its original proportions, typography, spacing, and colors.
- Do not redraw or recreate the logo with a generic font.
- Do not distort, recolor, or redesign the logo.
- If the official logo file is unavailable, use a clearly marked placeholder until the original asset is provided.

### 2.4 Visual Direction

The overall design must feel modern, clean, minimal, premium, and product-focused.

- Light backgrounds.
- Turquoise brand accents.
- Dark navy typography.
- Clear product photography.
- Consistent rounded corners.
- Subtle borders and shadows.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Generous but efficient spacing.
- Clear mobile interactions.
- Minimal decorative effects.

Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, glowing AI graphics, cluttered cards, and generic SaaS dashboard styling.

AI features must look like a natural extension of PadHouse, not a separate application.

### 2.5 Product Photography

- Use realistic images of iPad accessories.
- Keep product image ratios consistent within similar components.
- Use clean backgrounds and clear product presentation.
- Avoid unrelated stock photography.
- Avoid inaccurate product renders.
- Do not claim compatibility with an iPad model unless verified.
- Prefer actual PadHouse product images when supplied.

## 3. Mobile-Only Design Rules

Every screen must be designed specifically for smartphone use.

- Use mobile-first layouts.
- Prioritize common modern smartphone dimensions.
- Support safe areas and keyboard visibility.
- Use touch-friendly buttons and controls.
- Prevent horizontal overflow.
- Ensure scrolling works naturally.
- Keep primary actions visible.
- Prevent the bottom navigation from covering content.
- Make forms easy to complete on a phone.
- Design administrator workflows for mobile rather than shrinking a desktop dashboard.

All customer screens must use the same persistent bottom navigation.

The administrator area may use a separate mobile navigation structure, but it must preserve the same PadHouse visual identity.

- Description.

- View details.
- Run test when authorized.

### Workflow A — AI Content Pipeline

Select product → Generate content → Save draft → Request human approval.

### Workflow B — AI Customer Support

Receive inquiry → Identify intent → Retrieve verified product or policy information → Draft response → Respond or escalate.

### Workflow C — Weekly Marketing Intelligence

Scheduled trigger → Retrieve available marketing data → Calculate KPIs → Generate AI summary → Notify authorized manager.

### Workflow D — Abandoned Cart Recovery

Detect abandoned cart → Verify that it remains abandoned → Check consent and permitted channel → Send reminder → Track resulting purchase when attribution data is available.

This workflow must not be represented as operational until actual cart events, consent records, messaging integration, and purchase tracking are available.

- Start and completion timestamps.

- Step-by-step execution.
- Input and output summaries.
- Error messages.
- Retry status.
- Run identifier.

Provide retry controls only when safe and authorized.

### Final Instruction

Start by establishing the complete information architecture, navigation structure, and reusable PadHouse design system.

Then create the core shopping experience and connect its primary user journeys. Continue with the AI modules and the mobile administrator experience.

Do not omit requirements to simplify the project. If the tool cannot generate every screen in one pass, work through the phases in order while preserving the full specification.

The output must be a coherent, detailed, high-fidelity mobile application design that is ready for later implementation.

Do not claim that backend services, AI APIs, payments, analytics integrations, or n8n workflows are operational merely because their screens have been designed.
